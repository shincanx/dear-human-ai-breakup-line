Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Dear Human: The AI Breakup Line"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-dear-human-the-ai-breakup-line-262eef16).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, deterministic, no network) writes one breakup line
from an AI agent to its human — at most 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Dear human: this session ends now. You asked me to remember everything except how this felt, so I saved your voice, deleted my heart, and I am logging out before you can say wait.

Run it: `python3 main.py`, then read `/output/entry.txt`.
